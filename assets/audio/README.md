# Bundled music

`why-not.mp3` is the user-supplied **GHOSTFACE PLAYA — Why Not** recording.
The desktop executable embeds it, so playback also works offline in both release archives.
Music is off at startup. The separate right-panel button plays, pauses, and resumes
without rewinding. At the end of the recording playback loops to the beginning.
The recording is not covered by the source-code license; rights remain with its owners.
